What is SAS

Academic support,
built by students.

Student Academic Support (SAS) is a student-led tutoring and academic support initiative created to help students who may be struggling with certain subjects or topics in school.

The project aims to provide a supportive learning environment where students receive additional guidance from peers in a more comfortable and approachable setting — very different from the traditional classroom.

We identify common academic difficulties through surveys and feedback, then organise structured tutoring sessions tailored to those specific needs. The goal is simple: students helping students succeed.

How it works

Three steps to better
understanding.

01

Identify the needs

We collect feedback and surveys to understand which subjects and topics students find most challenging, then plan sessions around those priorities.

02

Organised sessions

Structured tutoring sessions are arranged based on identified needs — small groups, interactive, designed to encourage questions and participation.

03

Peer-to-peer support

Student tutors guide peers through concepts, homework, and exam prep in a relaxed setting — making it easy to ask questions without hesitation.

What we offer

Support that fits
how you learn.

Concepts

Explaining difficult topics

Struggling to understand something from class? Our peer tutors break it down using different approaches until it clicks — at your pace, in your language.

Homework

Homework guidance

Get help working through assignments and exercises. We help you understand the method — not just give you the answers — so you can do it yourself next time.

Exams

Exam revision sessions

Structured revision to cover key topics, tackle past questions, and strengthen weak areas before assessments and examinations.

Practice

Practice & application

Work through practice questions and real problems together. Build confidence through repetition and targeted feedback from peers who've been there.

Why it matters

Good for everyone
involved.

For students seeking help

A safe, comfortable space to learn without the pressure of a classroom.

  • Ask questions freely, without fear of judgment
  • Catch up on topics you missed or found confusing
  • Build understanding at your own pace
  • Improve grades and exam performance
  • Gain confidence in your own abilities

For student tutors

Teaching others is one of the most powerful ways to deepen your own understanding.

  • Develop leadership and communication skills
  • Strengthen your own subject knowledge
  • Build teamwork and mentoring experience
  • Contribute meaningfully to the school community
  • Grow skills that matter far beyond school
